Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · App version 1.0

Pedalwise is developed by Julian Sainz Martinez (Jelluna). The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking. Your bikes, rides, maintenance history and places are stored on your iPhone and, if you keep iCloud backup on, in your own private iCloud. The only server we run is a small routing relay, described under Route planning, which forwards route requests and keeps no record of them.

Stored on your device

Pedalwise stores what you enter so the app can work: your bikes and their specifications (for example motor, torque, battery, weights, tires, brakes), your riding preferences, maintenance tasks and service history, rides you log or finish, saved places such as Home and Work, recent destinations, your commute days and times, starred routes, the spots where you confirmed you waited at a traffic light (so future estimates include them), commute streaks and points, and settings. To save requests, the app also keeps recently planned routes for up to seven days and the latest weather forecasts for Home and Work in its cache. This information stays on your iPhone. A copy is also kept in your own private iCloud while iCloud backup is on (see below), and it is part of your device backups under your Apple settings.

iCloud backup

With iCloud backup on (My Bike → Settings → iCloud backup, on by default), Pedalwise keeps a copy of the information above, including your rides' routes and heart rate, in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account, so a reinstall or a new iPhone can restore it. Apple stores and encrypts it under Apple's privacy policy, it counts against your iCloud storage, and only your Apple Account can access it: the developer cannot see or read it. You can turn it off at any time. Erase all data deletes the copy in iCloud too.

Route planning

To calculate routes, Pedalwise sends the start and destination coordinates of the trip (during a ride, your current position if you reroute), a few routing preferences (such as bike type, typical speed and how much you prefer bike lanes or avoid hills) and the resulting route shape to our routing relay, a small service we run on Cloudflare. The relay passes them to our routing provider, Stadia Maps, on its servers in the European Union, and returns the route. The relay holds our access key, so the key never ships inside the app, and it doesn't store or log the requests. Requests do not include your name, Apple Account, bike name, maintenance data or ride history.

So that only the genuine Pedalwise app can use the relay, the app uses Apple's App Attest: once per installation it creates a key in your iPhone's Secure Enclave and Apple confirms it belongs to Pedalwise. The relay keeps that key's public part and its random identifier, which aren't linked to you, your Apple Account or anything else, so it can check that each route request comes from the app.

Cloudflare, Inc. and Stadia Maps, Inc. are based in the United States. They process requests to deliver and protect their services and may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time, under the Cloudflare and Stadia Maps privacy policies.

Weather

If you set Home or Work, Pedalwise asks Apple Weather for hourly forecasts at those two places so it can warn you about rain on your commute and suggest when to leave. Only the coordinates are sent, through Apple's WeatherKit, without your name, Apple Account or any identifier from the app. Apple processes these requests under Apple's privacy policy. Forecasts are refreshed in the background before your usual departure and kept on your iPhone.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings and give permission, Pedalwise saves each finished ride to Health as a cycling workout with its distance, duration, route and the calories you burned (on an e-bike, the motor's share is left out). On iPhone, Pedalwise writes these workouts and, after rides with heart rate from the watch and enough steady riding, an estimate of your VO₂ max; the only thing it reads is your resting heart rate, to make that estimate. If you use the Apple Watch app, the watch records your ride as a workout and reads your heart rate during it, to show it on your wrist and save it with the workout (so Fitness can show heart rate zones); you can also rate how hard the ride felt, which is saved with the workout. Health data is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is not used for advertising or any purpose other than recording your rides. You can change or revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings or the Health app.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app shows your next turn, speed, arrival time, battery, heart rate and upcoming maintenance, and asks the red-light question. This information travels directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch and is not sent to any server. With your permission the watch uses location during a ride to record the workout route in Apple Health. During a ride, the watch also sends your current heart rate to your iPhone every few seconds, so the ride screen can show your heart rate zone. You can start a ride to work, a ride home or a free ride on the watch; your iPhone then plans and follows it. When a ride ends, the watch sends that ride's heart rate (average, maximum and a reading about every ten seconds) and calories to your iPhone, where Pedalwise keeps them with the ride in your ride history, together with the route you rode and any VO₂ max estimate, so you can see your heart rate zones later. They stay on your iPhone (and in your private iCloud while iCloud backup is on), are never sent to the developer or any third party, and are deleted with the ride or when you erase all data.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your iPhone's location while it is open to plan routes from where you are and to follow your progress during a ride. If you turn on Start rides from your watch, iOS asks to allow location “Always”: Pedalwise then uses it only to start and follow a ride you begin on your Apple Watch while the iPhone stays in your pocket. Your location leaves your iPhone only as part of a route request, as described above. The route of each finished ride is kept in your ride history on your iPhone (and in your private iCloud while iCloud backup is on) so you can look at it later, and you can delete any ride. You can turn location access off in iPhone Settings and type a start point instead.

Maps and place search

The map, place search and address suggestions are provided by Apple MapKit. Search queries and map requests are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are handled by Apple. The app uses StoreKit to check whether Pedalwise Pro is active, its renewal date and whether you are eligible for a trial. Payment details are never shared with the developer.

Reminders

Maintenance, charging, rain and free-trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server.

Your control

You can edit or delete any bike, task or place in the app. My Bike → Settings → Erase all data removes everything Pedalwise stored on your device and its copy in your iCloud, including cached routes and forecasts, and its scheduled reminders. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. It does not affect your Apple subscription, files you exported (such as GPX routes) or your device backups.

This website and support

This website is hosted on Cloudflare and has no analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies. Cloudflare processes technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

If you email hello@jelluna.com, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to respond to you. Email is hosted by Apple iCloud Mail. Messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to access, correct or delete what you sent.

Children

Pedalwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, email hello@jelluna.com. If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
