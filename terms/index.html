Subscriptions

Pedalwise Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed monthly or yearly through your Apple Account. Eligible new subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

Riding safely

Routes, ride times, battery use, tire pressures, saddle heights and maintenance intervals are estimates and general guidance. They can be wrong. Always watch the road, obey local traffic laws and signs, and use your own judgment about whether a route, surface or maneuver is safe. Pedalwise never recommends running red lights. Do not use your phone in a way that distracts you while riding.

Maintenance guides are for simple checks. If you are unsure about anything that affects braking, steering, wheels or the battery, have a qualified bike shop do the work. Never exceed the pressure printed on your tire or rim.
